Final prompt:

Create ONE photographic contact sheet in exactly three equal horizontal rows stacked vertically, overall portrait aspect ratio 1:2, each row a landscape 3:2 photograph. Precise equal rows, no gaps, no borders, no text, no logos, no watermark. Top row: dramatic aerial travel photograph of Kelingking Beach in Nusa Penida Bali, famous massive lush green dinosaur-shaped limestone headland curving down into turquoise ocean, small white sandy cove, deep blue sea in distance, center the promontory and cove for portrait cropping. Middle row: cinematic wide view of Mount Bromo volcanic caldera in Indonesia, foreground crater with plume of white steam, dark ridged ash mountains and tall conical green-blue volcano behind, sea of low clouds, soft morning blue sky. Bottom row: atmospheric Bali lake temple with an elegant tall tiered black pagoda on the left and smaller temple pavilions by still water, misty green mountains in background, overcast pale sky, wide composition. Natural premium travel photography, realistic textures, beautiful restrained colour, no people in foreground. One combined contact sheet, not a webpage.
