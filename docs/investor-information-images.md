## Section behavior

The six category tabs switch the banner and a topic-specific paragraph. Quick Links is selected initially. Arrow Up/Down and Home/End support keyboard navigation. Financials, News, and Quick Links use imagery based on the supplied screenshots; the other categories reuse these three images. Paragraphs are draft presentation copy. The illustrations do not represent live financial data.
