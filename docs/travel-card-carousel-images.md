## Behavior

The carousel starts with Azure Coast centered and advances every 2 seconds. Hovering a card or focusing a card pauses rotation. Leaving the cards resumes after a full 2 seconds. Clicking a card centers it. There are no navigation arrows, selection dots, or play/pause controls.
