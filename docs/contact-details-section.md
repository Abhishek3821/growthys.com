# Contact details section

Rendered below the contact hero on `/contact`. Includes three contact cards, opening hours, a lazy-loaded Google Maps embed showing London Eye, and an email/name/message inquiry form.

Phone, email, location and opening hours are reference-based placeholders, not verified Growthys business details. Phone and email cards scroll to the inquiry form; the location card scrolls to the map. The form provides native required-field and email validation, then shows an availability notice. It does not send or store messages.

The map loads from Google Maps when the section enters the viewport. No API key is stored in this project. The layout stacks cards and the lower columns on small screens.
