# Testimonials portrait artwork

Saved asset: `public/images/testimonial-portraits.webp`.

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool, optimized to WebP with Sharp. This is one four-column, two-row image atlas; CSS shows individual cells in the staggered portrait cards. The people are fictional decorative portraits, not identified customers or attributed testimonials.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single portrait photo atlas to be displayed as small individual profile cards in a website section. Generate ONE contact sheet arranged in an EXACT 4 COLUMN by 2 ROW grid, EIGHT equally sized rectangular cells, absolutely NO gaps, borders, padding, collage overlap, labels, text or UI. Overall aspect ratio 8:5 so every cell is a 4:5 vertical portrait. Each cell is a separate crisp natural candid editorial business portrait of a different fictional adult professional, waist/chest up, face centered in the top half with generous headroom so no heads are cut off. Layout row1 left to right: (1) South Asian man wearing glasses and charcoal blazer in a bright modern studio; (2) friendly bearded European man with short brown hair in black t-shirt in a warm cafe; (3) East Asian man with glasses wearing a light blue button-down shirt in a sunlit office; (4) Middle Eastern man with short dark hair wearing a teal polo shirt and folded arms in a creative workspace. Layout row2 left to right: (5) European man with short sandy hair wearing a charcoal business jacket in a light office; (6) smiling Latina woman with long dark hair wearing a tan blouse in a warmly lit office; (7) smiling fair-skinned blonde woman in beige blazer seated at a desk in a bright office; (8) smiling Black woman with curly dark hair wearing a rust brown blouse in a warm cafe. Authentic friendly expressions, realistic skin textures, soft natural window lighting, softly blurred diverse office backgrounds, premium editorial photo quality. All eight faces visibly distinct. This is only the photographs; no testimonial quotes, writing, headings, typography, buttons, watermarks or extra graphics.
