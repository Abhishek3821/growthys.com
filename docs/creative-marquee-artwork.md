# Creative marquee illustrations

Created using the built-in imagegen tool with transparency. The atlas is exported into nine WebP images using `scripts/prepare-creative-marquee-images.cjs`.

Assets saved under `public/images/digital-marketing/marquee/`: `headphones.webp`, `trophy.webp`, `shopping.webp`, `star.webp`, `microphone.webp`, `offers.webp`, `tennis.webp`, `megaphone.webp`, `approval.webp`.

Final prompt:

Create ONE transparent-background contact sheet of nine polished playful 3D objects, exactly 3 equal columns and 3 equal rows, overall square. Each object centered entirely within its own square tile with generous empty transparent margins, no overlap between tiles, no grid lines, no labels, no words or logos. Top row from left to right: (1) oversized glossy purple over-ear headphones with tiny colourful floating music notes, (2) shiny golden victory trophy cup with two curved handles and small confetti pieces, (3) colourful mint green and orange shopping bags with a small credit card. Middle row: (4) a puffy dimensional yellow five-point star with pink orange beveled edges and small sparkle accents, (5) a silver vintage desktop microphone with black mesh grille and violet knobs on a short silver stand, (6) a pink red retail discount tag with a large dark percent symbol. Bottom row: (7) a light blue tennis racket with blue net and small yellow tennis balls, (8) a glossy blue purple megaphone with small envelope and speech bubble accents, (9) a beige wooden rubber stamp resting beside a white approval document with abstract lines and a circled checkmark. Tactile plastic and metal textures, sophisticated clay render, soft studio light and delicate shadows, consistent camera angle, front three quarter views. Actual alpha transparency throughout canvas except objects; no backgrounds, no UI, no borders, no watermark. This is a single object atlas.
