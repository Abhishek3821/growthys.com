# Website feature illustrations

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. The generated sheet is exported into three WebP images with `scripts/prepare-website-feature-images.cjs`.

Assets:
- `public/images/website-design/features/insights.webp`
- `public/images/website-design/features/lead-generation.webp`
- `public/images/website-design/features/campaigns.webp`

Final prompt:

Create ONE contact sheet of three soft polished 3D clay illustrations in exactly three equal horizontal rows stacked vertically, overall portrait aspect ratio 1:2, each row landscape 3:2. Pure white background throughout, no separators, no text or lettering, no logos, no webpage UI. Each scene is entirely contained within its own third with generous white margins on all four sides so it can be exported as a separate image. Top row: a floating white analytics report board with a thick rolled violet blue header, colourful ascending bar chart, curved orange trend line, small pink info card, tiny gold coins and a turquoise checkmark badge. Middle row: a dark plum rounded upright lead collection dashboard with a violet browser-like top, abstract contact tiles, pastel pink and blue profile elements, small floating pink spheres being gathered into a purple funnel in front, clean minimal sculptural scene. Bottom row: an open purple laptop with overlapping teal blue marketing dashboards, large turquoise and violet megaphone coming out of the screen, floating small social media symbol tiles, abstract video play icon, small chart blocks. Soft diffuse light, delicate shadows, premium friendly SaaS illustration, lilac violet teal pink and apricot palette. Three independent centered scenes in one regularly spaced vertically stacked atlas.
