Final prompt:

Create ONE black and white photographic contact sheet, exactly 3 equal columns by 3 equal rows, perfectly aligned edge-to-edge with no gutters or frames. Overall square aspect ratio, each of nine tiles square. All photographs monochrome, timeless cinematic fine art street and nature photography, rich blacks, subtle film grain, no text, no logos, no UI. Each subject centered with ample room for portrait cropping. Top row left to right: 1 Manhattan Bridge seen from an old cobbled Brooklyn street between tall dark brick buildings, distant bridge; 2 vintage European street corner storefronts and a cylindrical newspaper kiosk, no readable lettering; 3 a row of antique waterfront lampposts receding into mist on a rainy promenade. Middle row: 4 a small shaggy white dog lying beside a dark shuttered doorway on a wet cobblestone alley at night; 5 striking sculptural weathered driftwood rising diagonally against a plain pale sky; 6 minimalist modern outdoor concrete staircase rising towards a sweeping curved architectural roof and a single slim street lamp, lots of empty sky. Bottom row: 7 a bicycle leaning against a stone wall on a rainy old European narrow street; 8 dramatic low angle photograph of the Elizabeth Tower Big Ben clock face in London against heavy clouds, entire clock visible, no cropped clock; 9 close up portrait of two zebras together showing their striped heads and necks. One combined contact sheet with nine evenly sized photographs, not a website mockup.
